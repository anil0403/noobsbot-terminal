# Code of conduct

We want noobsbot terminal to be a friendly place for everyone, whatever their age, body, ability, ethnicity, gender, identity, experience, nationality, religion or sexual orientation.

## What we expect

- Be kind and patient. Many people here do not speak English as their first language.
- Respect other opinions and experience. Disagree with ideas, not with people.
- Give and take feedback politely, and say sorry when you get something wrong.
- Think about what is best for the project and the people who use it.

## What is not okay

- insults, put-downs, or attacks on someone's person or beliefs
- sexual language or images, or unwanted sexual attention
- harassment, in public or in private
- publishing someone's private details (like their address or email) without their permission
- anything else that would be out of place in a professional setting

## Where this applies

Everywhere in this project: issues, pull requests, discussions, reviews and commits. It also applies when you speak for the project somewhere else.

## Report a problem

Email the maintainers:

- Anil Shrestha: dev.shresthaanil@gmail.com
- Sandesh Bhusal: bhusalsandesh0@gmail.com

You can also use **Report content** on any comment on GitHub. We read every report, and we keep the name of the person who reported it private.

## What we do about it

The maintainers decide what happens, based on how serious it is:

1. **A private warning**, explaining what was wrong.
2. **A short break** from the project, if it happens again.
3. **A ban** from the project, for serious or repeated problems.
